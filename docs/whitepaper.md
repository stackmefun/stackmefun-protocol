# StackMe Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** A friendly, loud, community-run corner of Solana where small positions pile up into something worth showing off.

**Mission.** Keep StackMe easy to read, easy to verify and hard to misunderstand. Every claim on the site has to be checkable, and every address has to match what the registry states.

StackMe is a community experiment. It makes no promise about price and nothing in this document is financial advice.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1 - Ignite.** Launch the front door, light up the live board and gather the first crew.

**Phase 2 - Layer up.** Open the perks shelf: art packs, playlists and the first community votes.

**Phase 3 - Open lanes.** Partner experiments, creator tools and shared treasury rules.

**Phase 4 - Skyline.** A mature, self-steering crew that keeps going without a central team.
